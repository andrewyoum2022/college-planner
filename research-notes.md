# Fall 2027 Admissions Research Notes

Updated: 2026-09-06

This public GitHub-only planner uses official sources first. Do not store private family information here.

## Verified Fall 2027 dates

### University of California
- Fall 2027 application opens: August 1, 2026
- Filing period: October 1–November 30, 2026
- Financial aid filing begins: October 1, 2026
- First-year admission decisions: March 1–31, 2027 (some campuses may release earlier)
- FAFSA/CADAA/GPA Verification deadline: March 2, 2027
- First-year SIR: May 1, 2027
- Final official transcripts: July 1, 2027
- Official AP/IB results: July 15, 2027
- Official source: https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/how-to-apply/applying-as-a-first-year/dates-and-deadlines.html

### MIT
- Early Action application: November 1, 2026
- EA decision: mid-December
- Regular Action application: January 4, 2027
- RA decision: mid-March
- EA financial aid: November 30, 2026
- RA financial aid: February 15, 2027
- Enrollment reply: May 1, 2027
- SAT or ACT required
- Official source: https://mitadmissions.org/apply/firstyear/deadlines-requirements/

### Yale
- Single-Choice Early Action: November 1, 2026
- SCEA decision: mid-December
- Regular Decision: January 2, 2027
- RD decision: late March
- Reply: May 1, 2027
- Official source: https://admissions.yale.edu/timelines

## Research principles
- Costs: use official 2026–27 tuition/housing/meal/fees when published; otherwise explicitly label 2025–26.
- Admissions: use each college's official first-year admissions pages.
- Academic fit: summarize undergraduate Physics, Philosophy, double-major/minor/secondary options and undergraduate research.
- Never present an estimated deadline as verified.
